About

From Shanghai University to the people's lawyer.

Before coming to the U.S., Li Wei taught International Law at Shanghai University. He graduated from Willamette University College of Law in 1997, where his thesis won first place in the school's International Law Article Competition.

Admitted to the Oregon State Bar in 1997 and the Washington State Bar in 2004, he founded the Law Office of Li Wei in 1999.
