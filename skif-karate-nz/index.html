About

Unify body, mind and spirit.

“Kara” means empty, “te” means hand and “Do” is the way. Karate-Do is the Way of the Empty Hand.

Through hard, proper training in a real Japanese dojo, our members achieve a physical and mental state of holistic wellbeing and harmony.
