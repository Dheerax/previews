About

Montavilla's mechanic-owned bike shop.

Oregon Bike Shop serves the cycling communities of Montavilla, Mt. Tabor and the whole City of Portland with fair sales and quality repairs.

We also run the Oregon Bike Shop Racing Team — riders from 9 to 60+, racing mountain bike, short track, cyclocross and plenty of gravel and road.
